## Description (draft)

**The Bible, Beautifully Spoken.**

Scripture Echo is the free audio Bible with a human voice.

**100% HUMAN — NO AI VOICES**
Every chapter is read aloud by a real human being. Never text-to-speech,
never a robot. The flagship American Standard Version is narrated by one
man — Ron Altman — from Genesis to Revelation: one voice, the whole Bible.

**COMPLETELY FREE**
No ads. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. No accounts. Free means free.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
No sign-up, no tracking, no analytics. Your bookmarks, history, and
downloads live only on your phone.

**READ AND LISTEN, ANYWHERE**
Three public-domain translations built into the app — King James Version,
World English Bible, and American Standard Version — reading works fully
offline. Download chapters for offline listening, with background playback,
lock-screen controls, playback speeds from 0.75x to 2x, sleep timer, and
resume right where you left off.

Midnight navy and warm gold. No feeds, no noise. Just the Word, beautifully
spoken.

Scripture Echo is a ministry of Real Scripture Path Inc. — realscripturepath.com
