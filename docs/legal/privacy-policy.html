Privacy Policy — Scripture Echo

Effective date: October 31, 2026 Real Scripture Path Inc. · realscripturepath.com · Support: fabrod035@gmail.com

Scripture Echo (the Scripture Path audio Bible app) is built on a simple conviction: your time in God's Word is between you and God. This app is designed to collect as little information as possible — ideally, none.

What we collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically:

  • No accounts. The app never asks you to sign up, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or phone number.
  • No tracking. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no social-media trackers.
  • No ads. Nothing in the app profiles you or serves advertising.

What stays on your device

The following are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage, and are never transmitted to us:

  • Bookmarks and favorite passages
  • Listening history and resume positions
  • Downloaded audio chapters
  • Your preferences (translation, narrator, playback speed, appearance)

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in Settings or by deleting the app.

Network requests and server logs

To deliver audio, the app downloads chapter files and a content manifest from our content hosting provider. As with any web server, the hosting provider's systems automatically record standard technical request logs — such as the requesting IP address, the date and time, and which file was requested — for security and operational reliability. We do not use these logs to identify, track, or profile users, and we do not sell or share them for advertising or marketing. Scripture text itself is bundled inside the app and reading works fully offline, with no network request at all.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The app contains no accounts, messaging, comments, or social features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the Privacy Policy URL in the App Store listing and inside the app, with a new effective date.
