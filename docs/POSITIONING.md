# POSITIONING — what makes Scripture Echo stand out

Decided in the build, 2026-10-31. Every claim below is true of this app and
verifiable in the product — no "first/only" superlatives beyond what we can
prove about ourselves.

## The wedge: 100% HUMAN.

The Bible-app market is flooding with robot Bibles — text-to-speech voices
reading Scripture like a weather report. This app takes the opposite stand:

1. **Every chapter is read by a real human being. Never AI. Never
   text-to-speech.** (User decision, 2026-10-31 — synthetic voices researched
   and rejected outright.)
2. **One voice for the whole Bible.** The flagship ASV is narrated by a
   single man — Ron Altman — Genesis to Revelation. Not a patchwork, not a
   dramatized cast with sound effects: one reader, the whole counsel of God.
3. **Completely free — and free means free.** No ads. No subscriptions. No
   in-app purchases. No accounts. No tracking. Most "free" Bible apps
   monetize attention or data; this one monetizes nothing.
4. **Your time in the Word is private.** Everything personal — bookmarks,
   history, downloads — lives only on the user's phone. There is nothing to
   leak, sell, or profile, because there is nothing collected.
5. **Owned, not licensed — so it can never be taken away.** Texts and
   recordings are public domain, hosted on infrastructure we control. No
   licensing change can ever pull the audio out of this app, and no API
   outage can silence it.
6. **Offline to the ends of the earth.** The full text of three translations
   is inside the app; chapters download for offline listening. Airplane,
   village, hospital, prison ministry, mission field — the Word plays.
7. **Peaceful by design.** Midnight navy and gold. No feeds, no streaks, no
   social, no notification games. Open it and there is one thing: the Word,
   read aloud like it matters.

## The one-line version

**The free audio Bible with a human voice — no AI, no ads, no accounts, no tracking.**

## Why it fits the studio

Real Scripture Path Inc. exists to help solve huge problems in a broken
world. A generation is being discipled by machines; putting a real human
voice reading the real Word into their pocket — free, private, and
un-silenceable — is exactly the kind of problem this studio was founded for.
