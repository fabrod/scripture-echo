# App Privacy Answers — Scripture Echo (App Store Connect questionnaire)

**Data collection: NONE.** Scripture Echo collects no data of any kind.

- Does the app collect data? **No.**
- Tracking: **No** — the app does not track users across apps or websites,
  contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and shares nothing with data
  brokers.
- Every App Store "data type" category: **Not Collected**
  (Contact info, Health, Financial, Location, Sensitive info, Contacts,
  User content, Browsing history, Search history, Identifiers, Purchases,
  Usage data, Diagnostics, Other — all Not Collected).

**Notes for the form:**
- Bookmarks, favorites, listening history, and settings are stored ONLY on
  the device (on-device SQLite) and never transmitted — per Apple's guidance
  this is not "collection."
- The app downloads audio files from our content host. Standard web-server
  request logs exist on the host (like any website); we do not use them to
  identify or profile users, and nothing is linked to identity. If the
  questionnaire's server-log guidance changes, this stays accurate because
  no data is linked, tracked, or shared.
- Privacy Policy URL: see app-store-listing.md (deploy docs/legal first).
